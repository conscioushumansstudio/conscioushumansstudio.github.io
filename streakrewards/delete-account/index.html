Delete your Streak Rewards account

You can delete your Streak Rewards account (by Conscious Humans Studio) and all its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Streak Rewards and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Edit profile.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom, and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out.

Without the app

Email support@conscioushumansstudio.com with the subject "Delete my account", from the email address you sign in with. We'll confirm and delete your account within 7 days.

What's deleted

  • Your account, name, email and profile photo
  • All habits, check-ins, goals and milestones
  • Your wishlist and rewards, including photos and shop links, and your claimed history
  • Unfreeze coins and plan history
  • Your analytics history

This can't be undone. Copies in our encrypted backups are removed automatically within 30 days.

What's kept

Nothing that identifies you. Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their policies.

Premium subscribers

Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel first in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings so you're not charged again. See Support for how.
