Support

Need a hand? We're happy to help.

Contact us

Email support@conscioushumansstudio.com. Please write from the email you sign in with, and tell us your phone model if something isn't working. We usually reply within 2 working days.

Common questions

I didn't get my sign-in code

Check your spam or promotions folder, and make sure the email address is spelt correctly. Codes expire after a few minutes, so tap Resend for a new one.

How do I cancel or change Premium?

In the app: Profile → Manage subscription → Manage in App Store / Google Play. Or open your phone's subscription settings: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. Premium stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.

I'm on a new phone and Premium isn't showing

Sign in with the same account, then go to Profile → Manage subscription → Restore purchases. Make sure the phone uses the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone, use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open Google Play → Payments and subscriptions → Budget and history, or contact Google Play support.

My habit froze. What happened?

A habit freezes when you go over the misses you allowed. Its reward freezes with it. Open the habit and tap Unfreeze for 1 coin to carry on. You get a free coin every Sunday.
