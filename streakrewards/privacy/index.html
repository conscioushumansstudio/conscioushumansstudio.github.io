Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

This policy explains what the Streak Rewards app ("the app") collects, why, who helps us run it, and the choices you have. The app is made by Conscious Humans Studio ("we", "us"). If anything here is unclear, write to privacy@conscioushumansstudio.com.

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your sign-in email, your name, and the habits, goals and rewards you add.
  • We don't sell your data, and the app shows no ads.
  • Usage analytics are tied to a random account id, never to your name or email.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time, in the app or by email.

2. Shop links

When you paste a shop link while adding a reward, our server opens that page once to read the product's name, price and photo for you. We don't add tracking to the link, and the shop doesn't learn who you are from this step. When you tap Buy now, the link opens in your browser or the shop's app, and that shop's own privacy policy applies from then on.

3. Analytics

We use PostHog to see how the app is used, for example how many people finish onboarding or keep a habit past 30 days. These events are linked only to your random account id. They never include your email, name, reward names, shop links or photos, and prices are sent only as rough bands (for example "1,000–5,000"). If we record app sessions to find bugs, all text you type and all images are hidden. When you log out, the app stops linking events to you, and when you delete your account, your analytics history is deleted too.

5. How long we keep data

  • We keep your data while your account exists, so your history stays with you. On the Free plan, older history is hidden in the app but not deleted, so it comes back if you upgrade.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your account, habits, goals, rewards, photos, coins and analytics history straight away. Copies in our encrypted backups are removed automatically within 30 days.
  • Apple and Google keep their own records of purchases under their policies.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See and edit: change your name, photo, email (email sign-in only), habits, goals and rewards in the app at any time.
  • Delete: Profile → Edit profile → Delete account, or see Delete your account.
  • Notifications: switch reminders off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Copy of your data, or other requests: email privacy@conscioushumansstudio.com from the email you sign in with. We reply within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the UK GDPR or EU GDPR, or Californian privacy law), you may have further rights, such as to object to processing, to correct data, or to complain to your local data protection authority. We'll help you use them. We process your data to provide the app you asked for (our contract with you), and for analytics and security where we have a legitimate interest in improving and protecting the app.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Each account can only read its own data, enforced on our server. Your sign-in session is stored in your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android).

8. Teens and children

Streak Rewards is for people aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13; if we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we delete it. If you believe this has happened, write to us.

For everyone, including teens: the app shows no ads, we don't profile anyone for advertising, and analytics never include your name, email, rewards or photos (see section 3).

If you're under 18, please ask your parent or guardian for permission before using Streak Rewards. Your parent or guardian can contact us at any time to see or delete your data. Premium purchases go through Apple or Google, so the family purchase controls on your phone (such as Ask to Buy or Google Family Link) apply.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.
